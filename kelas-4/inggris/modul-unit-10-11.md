## Unit 10: How Much Snow Is There?

### Soal 1
**Pertanyaan:** Rina looks out of the window. It is winter, and white snow covers the street. She asks her brother, "How much snow is there?" Which is the best answer?

A. "There are three snow."
B. "There is a lot of snow."
C. "There is a snowman."
D. "It is summer."

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Pertanyaan "How much snow is there?" menanyakan jumlah salju. Salju (snow) adalah benda yang tidak bisa dihitung satu per satu, jadi jawabannya memakai "There is a lot of snow" (ada banyak salju). Pilihan A salah karena "snow" tidak bisa dihitung dengan angka; C menjawab dengan benda lain (snowman); D salah karena musimnya winter, bukan summer.

### Soal 2
**Pertanyaan:** Dodi says, "How many snow is there?" What is wrong with his sentence?

A. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
B. He should say, "How many snows are there?"
C. He should say, "How much snow is there?" "Snow" cannot be counted, so we use "how much", not "how many".
D. He should say, "How much snowman is there?"

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** "How many" dipakai untuk benda yang bisa dihitung (misalnya "How many snowmen are there?"), sedangkan "how much" dipakai untuk benda yang tidak bisa dihitung satu per satu, seperti snow, rain, dan water. Jadi kalimat Dodi yang benar adalah "How much snow is there?"

### Soal 3
**Pertanyaan:** In City A, there is a little snow on the ground. In City B, there is a lot of snow, and the snow is very deep. In which city can the children build a big snowman?

A. City A, because a little snow is enough for a big snowman.
B. Neither city, because snowmen are built in summer.
C. Both cities, because snowmen do not need snow.
D. City B, because a lot of snow is needed to build a big snowman.

**Jawaban benar:** D

**Pembahasan:** "A little snow" berarti salju sedikit, sedangkan "a lot of snow" berarti salju banyak. Untuk membuat boneka salju yang besar (big snowman), anak-anak butuh banyak salju — jadi Kota B yang tepat. Boneka salju dibuat di musim dingin (winter), bukan di musim panas (summer).

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 10: How Much Snow Is There?

### Soal 4
**Pertanyaan:** It is winter in Canada. The weather is cold and snowy. Sinta wants to play outside with her friends. What should she wear, and what can they do outside?

A. She should wear a warm jacket and boots, and they can build a snowman.
B. She should wear shorts and a T-shirt, and they can swim in the sea.
C. She should wear a raincoat, because it is sunny.
D. She should stay inside, because children never play in winter.

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Musim dingin (winter) itu dingin dan bersalju (cold and snowy), jadi Sinta harus memakai pakaian hangat seperti jaket tebal (warm jacket) dan sepatu bot (boots). Kegiatan yang cocok di musim dingin bersalju adalah membuat boneka salju (build a snowman). Pilihan B cocok untuk musim panas di pantai, C salah karena raincoat dipakai saat hujan bukan saat cerah, dan D salah karena anak-anak tetap bisa bermain di musim dingin dengan pakaian yang tepat.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 10: How Much Snow Is There?

### Soal 5
**Pertanyaan:** What does "snowy" mean?

A. Cerah
B. Bersalju
C. Berangin
D. Berawan

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** "Snowy" berarti bersalju, dari kata "snow" (salju). "Sunny" berarti cerah, "windy" berarti berangin, dan "cloudy" berarti berawan.

## Unit 11: Do You Know How to Play Chess?

### Soal 6
**Pertanyaan:** Rina asks Budi, "Do you know how to play chess?" Budi can play chess very well. Which is the best answer for Budi?

A. "No, I don't. I can't play chess."
B. "Yes, I do. I can play chess."
C. "Yes, I am. I play chess."
D. "No, I am not. I play football."

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Pertanyaan "Do you know how to play chess?" menanyakan apakah seseorang bisa bermain catur, jadi dijawab dengan "Yes, I do" kalau bisa, atau "No, I don't" kalau tidak bisa. Budi bisa bermain catur dengan baik, jadi jawaban yang benar adalah "Yes, I do. I can play chess." Pilihan C dan D salah karena pertanyaan "Do you ...?" tidak dijawab dengan "Yes, I am" atau "No, I am not".

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 11: Do You Know How to Play Chess?

### Soal 7
**Pertanyaan:** Dodi says, "Do you know how play chess?" What is wrong with his sentence?

A. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
B. He should say, "Do you know how to plays chess?"
C. He should say, "Do you know how to play chess?" After "how" we use "to" before the verb.
D. He should say, "Do you knows how to play chess?"

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Pola yang benar adalah "Do you know how to play ...?" — setelah kata "how" harus ada "to" sebelum kata kerja. Jadi kalimat Dodi yang benar adalah "Do you know how to play chess?" Pilihan B salah karena "plays" tidak dipakai setelah "to", dan D salah karena setelah "Do you" kata kerjanya "know", bukan "knows".

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 11: Do You Know How to Play Chess?

### Soal 8
**Pertanyaan:** Read about two games.

Chess is played on a board with pieces. Football is played on a field with a ball.

Which sentence is true?

A. Chess is played on a board, and football is played on a field with a ball.
B. Chess is played on a field, and football is played on a board.
C. Chess and football are both played with a ball.
D. Chess and football are both played on a board.

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Catur (chess) dimainkan di atas papan (board) dengan bidak (pieces), sedangkan sepak bola (football) dimainkan di lapangan (field) dengan bola (ball). Jadi hanya pilihan A yang benar. Pilihan B menukar tempatnya, C salah karena catur tidak memakai bola, dan D salah karena sepak bola tidak dimainkan di papan.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 11: Do You Know How to Play Chess?

### Soal 9
**Pertanyaan:** Sinta plays a game. She hits a shuttlecock with a racket over a net. What game does Sinta play?

A. Chess
B. Football
C. Hide and seek
D. Badminton

**Jawaban benar:** D

**Pembahasan:** Permainan yang dimainkan dengan memukul shuttlecock (kok) memakai raket melewati net adalah badminton (bulu tangkis). Catur (chess) dimainkan dengan bidak di papan, sepak bola (football) dimainkan dengan menendang bola, dan hide and seek (petak umpet) adalah permainan bersembunyi — ketiganya tidak memakai raket dan shuttlecock.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 11: Do You Know How to Play Chess?

### Soal 10
**Pertanyaan:** In hide and seek, one child closes his eyes and counts, and the other children hide. What is the game?

A. Chess
B. Hide and seek
C. Badminton
D. Football

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Hide and seek (petak umpet) adalah permainan di mana satu anak menutup mata dan berhitung, sementara anak-anak lain bersembunyi. Catur (chess) dimainkan dengan bidak di papan, badminton dimainkan dengan raket dan shuttlecock, dan sepak bola (football) dimainkan dengan menendang bola — ketiganya bukan permainan bersembunyi.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 11: Do You Know How to Play Chess?
