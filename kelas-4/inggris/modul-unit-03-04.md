## Unit 3: Do You Like Listening to Jazz or Pop?

### Soal 1
**Pertanyaan:** Read the dialogue.

Rina: "Do you like listening to jazz or pop?"
Dina: "____"

Which is the best answer for Dina? She likes jazz music.

A. "I'm from Jakarta."
B. "I am nine years old."
C. "I like jazz."
D. "I like reading books."

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Pertanyaan "Do you like listening to jazz or pop?" menanyakan kesukaan terhadap musik, dan Dina suka jazz. Jawaban yang tepat adalah "I like jazz." Pilihan A menjawab asal, B menjawab umur, dan D menjawab kesukaan lain (membaca buku) — ketiganya tidak menjawab pertanyaan tentang jazz atau pop.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 3: Do You Like Listening to Jazz or Pop?

### Soal 2
**Pertanyaan:** Raka says, "She don't like listening to rock music." What is wrong with his sentence?

A. "like" should be "likes", because the subject is "she"
B. "listening" should be "listen", because we cannot use verb-ing after "like"
C. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
D. "don't" should be "doesn't", because the subject is "she"

**Jawaban benar:** D

**Pembahasan:** Untuk subjek "she", bentuk negatif yang benar adalah "doesn't" (does not), bukan "don't". Kalimat yang benar: "She doesn't like listening to rock music." Kata "like" tetap bentuk dasar setelah "doesn't", dan "listening" tetap memakai verb-ing setelah "like" — jadi pilihan A dan B salah.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 3: Do You Like Listening to Jazz or Pop?

### Soal 3
**Pertanyaan:** Budi says, "I like listening to jazz." Sinta says, "I like listening to pop." Who likes jazz?

A. Sinta
B. Budi
C. Both of them
D. Nobody

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Budi berkata ia suka mendengarkan jazz, sedangkan Sinta berkata ia suka mendengarkan pop. Jadi, yang suka jazz adalah Budi, bukan Sinta.

### Soal 4
**Pertanyaan:** Rani sings pop songs every day. She listens to pop music at home and at school. What does Rani like?

A. Listening to jazz
B. Listening to pop
C. Listening to rock
D. Listening to dangdut

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Rani menyanyikan lagu pop setiap hari dan selalu mendengarkan musik pop di rumah dan di sekolah. Perilakunya menunjukkan bahwa ia suka mendengarkan pop, bukan jazz, rock, atau dangdut.

### Soal 5
**Pertanyaan:** Which one is a kind of music?

A. Book
B. Ball
C. Table
D. Jazz

**Jawaban benar:** D

**Pembahasan:** Jazz adalah salah satu genre musik, seperti pop, rock, dan dangdut. "Book" (buku), "ball" (bola), dan "table" (meja) bukan jenis musik.

## Unit 4: Did You Have a Good Time?

### Soal 6
**Pertanyaan:** Read the dialogue.

Rina: "Did you have a good time at the zoo yesterday?"
Budi: "____"

Which is the best answer for Budi?

A. "Yes, I do. I like the zoo."
B. "No, I don't. I don't like the zoo."
C. "Yes, I did. I saw the elephants and I was very happy."
D. "Yes, I am. I am happy."

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Pertanyaan "Did you have a good time ... yesterday?" menanyakan pengalaman di masa lalu, jadi jawabannya memakai bentuk past: "Yes, I did" atau "No, I didn't". Pilihan C benar karena memakai "Yes, I did" dan menjelaskan pengalamannya kemarin. Pilihan A dan B memakai "do/don't" (bentuk sekarang), sedangkan D memakai "am" — ketiganya tidak cocok untuk pertanyaan tentang kemarin.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 4: Did You Have a Good Time?

### Soal 7
**Pertanyaan:** Dodi says, "I didn't went to the zoo last Sunday." What is wrong with his sentence?

A. He should say "I didn't go to the zoo last Sunday", because after "didn't" the verb is in the base form, not the past form.
B. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
C. He should say "I don't went to the zoo last Sunday."
D. He should say "I didn't going to the zoo last Sunday."

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Setelah kata "didn't", kata kerja harus memakai bentuk dasar (base form), bukan bentuk past. "Went" adalah bentuk past dari "go", jadi "didn't went" salah. Kalimat yang benar adalah "I didn't go to the zoo last Sunday."

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 4: Did You Have a Good Time?

### Soal 8
**Pertanyaan:** Last Sunday, Rani went to the beach. She swam and played with her friends. Budi stayed at home. He watched TV all day. Who had a good time at the beach?

A. Budi, because he watched TV all day.
B. Rani, because she went to the beach and played with her friends.
C. Budi, because he stayed at home.
D. Nobody, because they stayed at home.

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Rani pergi ke pantai (went to the beach), berenang, dan bermain dengan teman-temannya. Budi tinggal di rumah dan menonton TV. Jadi yang bersenang-senang di pantai adalah Rani, bukan Budi.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 4: Did You Have a Good Time?

### Soal 9
**Pertanyaan:** Last week, Sinta and her family ____ her grandmother in the village. They had a good time together.

Which verb is correct to complete the sentence?

A. visit
B. visiting
C. visits
D. visited

**Jawaban benar:** D

**Pembahasan:** Kalimat ini bercerita tentang minggu lalu ("last week"), jadi kata kerjanya harus bentuk past. Bentuk past dari "visit" adalah "visited". Pilihan A "visit" dan C "visits" adalah bentuk sekarang, sedangkan B "visiting" dipakai untuk kalimat yang sedang berlangsung, bukan untuk cerita minggu lalu.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 4: Did You Have a Good Time?

### Soal 10
**Pertanyaan:** What is the past form of "go"?

A. Goes
B. Going
C. Went
D. Gone to school

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** "Go" adalah kata kerja tak beraturan (irregular verb). Bentuk past-nya adalah "went", misalnya "I went to school yesterday." "Goes" adalah bentuk sekarang untuk he/she/it, "going" dipakai untuk kalimat sedang berlangsung, dan "gone to school" bukan bentuk past yang tepat di sini.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 4: Did You Have a Good Time?
