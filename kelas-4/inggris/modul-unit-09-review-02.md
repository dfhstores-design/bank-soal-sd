## Unit 9: He's Good at Surfing

### Soal 1
**Pertanyaan:** Budi can stand on a surfboard and ride big waves in the sea. Which sentence is true about Budi?

A. He is good at swimming.
B. He is good at surfing.
C. He is good at dancing.
D. He can't surf.

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Budi bisa berdiri di atas papan selancar dan menunggangi ombak besar — itu artinya ia pandai berselancar, jadi kalimat yang benar adalah "He is good at surfing." Pilihan A salah karena berenang (swimming) bukan berselancar, C salah karena menari (dancing) tidak ada hubungannya, dan D bertentangan dengan fakta bahwa Budi bisa berselancar.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 9: He's Good at Surfing

### Soal 2
**Pertanyaan:** Dodi says, "I am good at swim." What is wrong with his sentence?

A. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
B. He should say, "I am good at swimming." After "good at" we use a verb with -ing.
C. He should say, "I am good at swims."
D. He should say, "I can swim good."

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Setelah "good at" harus memakai kata kerja bentuk -ing, jadi yang benar adalah "I am good at swimming", bukan "good at swim". Pilihan C salah karena "swims" bukan bentuk -ing, dan D salah karena "good" di sini harusnya "well" ("I can swim well") — lagipula D tidak memperbaiki kesalahan pola "good at".

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 9: He's Good at Surfing

### Soal 3
**Pertanyaan:** Read about two children.

Rani is good at swimming, but she can't ride a bike.
Budi is good at riding a bike, but he can't swim.

The swimming club needs a new member. Who can join the club?

A. Rani, because she is good at swimming.
B. Budi, because he is good at riding a bike.
C. Both of them, because they are both good at sports.
D. Nobody, because they can't swim.

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Klub renang membutuhkan anggota yang bisa berenang. Rani pandai berenang ("good at swimming"), jadi ia bisa bergabung. Budi pandai naik sepeda tetapi tidak bisa berenang ("he can't swim"), jadi ia tidak cocok untuk klub renang.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 9: He's Good at Surfing

### Soal 4
**Pertanyaan:** Sinta can draw beautiful pictures, but she can't sing a song. Which sentence is correct about Sinta?

A. She can sing, but she can't draw.
B. She can't draw and she can't sing.
C. She can draw, but she can't sing.
D. She can sing and draw.

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Sinta bisa menggambar ("can draw") tetapi tidak bisa bernyanyi ("can't sing"), jadi kalimat yang benar adalah "She can draw, but she can't sing." Pilihan A membalik faktanya, B salah karena Sinta bisa menggambar, dan D salah karena Sinta tidak bisa bernyanyi.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 9: He's Good at Surfing

### Soal 5
**Pertanyaan:** A person who stands on a board and rides the waves in the sea is a ___.

A. Singer
B. Surfer
C. Teacher
D. Farmer

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Orang yang berdiri di atas papan dan menunggangi ombak di laut disebut "surfer" (peselancar). "Singer" adalah penyanyi, "teacher" adalah guru, dan "farmer" adalah petani — ketiganya bukan orang yang berselancar.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 9: He's Good at Surfing


## Review 2 (Units 5–8)

### Soal 6
**Pertanyaan:** Read what Rina says.

"I like English the most, but I don't like Math."

Which sentence is true about Rina?

A. "Rina likes Math the most."
B. "Rina likes English the most."
C. "Rina likes English and Math the same."
D. "Rina doesn't like English."

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Rina berkata "I like English the most" — English adalah mata pelajaran yang paling ia sukai (Unit 5). Ia juga berkata "I don't like Math", jadi pilihan A salah, dan C salah karena ia tidak menyukai keduanya sama. Pilihan D juga salah karena ia justru paling suka English.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 2 (Unit 5: What Subject Do You Like the Most?)

---

### Soal 7
**Pertanyaan:** Which sentence is a school rule?

A. "You must be late."
B. "You mustn't listen to the teacher."
C. "You must shout in class."
D. "You must do your homework."

**Jawaban benar:** D

**Pembahasan:** Aturan sekolah memakai "must" untuk kewajiban dan "mustn't" untuk larangan (Unit 6). "You must do your homework" adalah kewajiban yang benar. Pilihan A salah karena aturannya "You mustn't be late" — kamu tidak boleh terlambat. Pilihan B salah karena mendengarkan guru itu wajib: "You must listen to the teacher". Pilihan C salah karena aturannya "You mustn't shout in class".

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 2 (Unit 6: You Must Be on Time)

---

### Soal 8
**Pertanyaan:** Which sentence is correct?

A. "I want to be an farmer."
B. "I want to be a artist."
C. "I want to be a police officer."
D. "I want to be an teacher."

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Kata "a" dipakai sebelum kata yang diawali bunyi konsonan, dan "an" sebelum kata yang diawali bunyi vokal (Unit 7). "Farmer" diawali bunyi konsonan (f), jadi harus "a farmer" — pilihan A salah. "Artist" diawali bunyi vokal (a), jadi harus "an artist" — pilihan B salah. "Teacher" diawali bunyi konsonan (t), jadi harus "a teacher" — pilihan D salah. Hanya pilihan C yang benar: "a police officer" karena "police" diawali bunyi konsonan (p).

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 2 (Unit 7: What Do You Want to Be?)

### Soal 9
**Pertanyaan:** Read the dialogue.

Teacher: "Why do you want to be a pilot, Dodi?"
Dodi: "____"

Which is the best answer for Dodi?

A. "Because I like flying in the sky."
B. "Because I like cooking."
C. "Because I like sleeping."
D. "Because I like swimming."

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Pertanyaan "Why ...?" dijawab dengan "Because ..." yang alasannya cocok dengan profesi itu (Unit 8). Pilot menerbangkan pesawat di langit, jadi alasan yang cocok adalah "Because I like flying in the sky". Pilihan B cocok untuk chef, C bukan alasan memilih profesi, dan D cocok untuk swimmer — ketiganya tidak cocok untuk pilot.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 2 (Unit 8: Why Do You Want to Be a Singer?)

### Soal 10
**Pertanyaan:** Read about Rani.

Rani likes Science the most. She wants to be a doctor because she likes helping sick people.

Which sentence is true about Rani?

A. "Rani likes Math the most."
B. "Rani wants to be a teacher."
C. "Rani likes Science the most and wants to be a doctor."
D. "Rani wants to be a singer."

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Soal ini menggabungkan dua unit. Dari Unit 5: Rani paling suka Science ("I like Science the most"), bukan Math — jadi pilihan A salah. Dari Unit 7 dan 8: Rani ingin menjadi doctor karena suka menolong orang sakit ("I want to be a doctor because I like helping sick people") — jadi pilihan B dan D salah. Hanya pilihan C yang benar, karena menyebut kedua fakta itu sekaligus.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 2 (Unit 5: What Subject Do You Like the Most? + Unit 7: What Do You Want to Be?)
