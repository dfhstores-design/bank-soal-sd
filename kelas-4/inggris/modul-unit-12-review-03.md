## Unit 12: How Often Do You Do the Laundry?

### Soal 1
**Pertanyaan:** Rina asks Budi, "How often do you do the laundry, Budi?" Which is the best answer for Budi?

A. "I am nine years old."
B. "I do it twice a week."
C. "My name is Budi."
D. "I like apples."

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Pertanyaan "How often do you do the laundry?" menanyakan seberapa sering Budi mencuci pakaian, jadi dijawab dengan frekuensi, misalnya "I do it twice a week" (seminggu dua kali). Pilihan A menjawab umur, C menjawab nama, D menjawab kesukaan — ketiganya tidak menjawab pertanyaan tentang frekuensi.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 12: How Often Do You Do the Laundry?

### Soal 2
**Pertanyaan:** Dodi says, "I do always the laundry on Sunday." What is wrong with his sentence?

A. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
B. He should say, "I always does the laundry on Sunday."
C. He should say, "I always do the laundry on Sunday." The adverb "always" comes before the main verb.
D. He should say, "I do the laundry on always Sunday."

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Kata keterangan frekuensi seperti "always" diletakkan sebelum kata kerja utama, jadi yang benar adalah "I always do the laundry on Sunday", bukan "I do always the laundry". Pilihan B salah karena "does" dipakai untuk he/she/it, bukan untuk "I". Pilihan D salah karena "always" tidak diletakkan setelah kata "on".

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 12: How Often Do You Do the Laundry?

### Soal 3
**Pertanyaan:** Rani does the laundry twice a week. Budi does the laundry once a week. Who does the laundry more often?

A. Rani, because twice a week is more often than once a week.
B. Budi, because once a week is more often than twice a week.
C. Both of them, because they do it the same number of times.
D. Nobody, because they never do the laundry.

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** "Twice a week" berarti dua kali dalam seminggu, sedangkan "once a week" berarti satu kali dalam seminggu. Dua kali lebih sering daripada satu kali, jadi Rani mencuci pakaian lebih sering daripada Budi.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 12: How Often Do You Do the Laundry?

### Soal 4
**Pertanyaan:** Sinta waters the plants every day. Which sentence is true about Sinta?

A. She never waters the plants.
B. She sometimes waters the plants.
C. She rarely waters the plants.
D. She always waters the plants.

**Jawaban benar:** D

**Pembahasan:** "Always" berarti selalu — setiap kali, tanpa kecuali. Sinta menyiram tanaman setiap hari (every day), jadi kalimat yang benar adalah "She always waters the plants". Pilihan A salah karena "never" berarti tidak pernah, B salah karena "sometimes" berarti hanya kadang-kadang, dan C salah karena "rarely" berarti jarang.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 12: How Often Do You Do the Laundry?

### Soal 5
**Pertanyaan:** Rina cleans the floor with a broom every morning. What does Rina do every morning?

A. She washes the dishes.
B. She sweeps the floor.
C. She makes the bed.
D. She waters the plants.

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Membersihkan lantai dengan sapu dalam Bahasa Inggris disebut "sweep the floor" (menyapu lantai). "Wash the dishes" berarti mencuci piring, "make the bed" berarti merapikan tempat tidur, dan "water the plants" berarti menyiram tanaman — ketiganya bukan pekerjaan Rina di soal ini.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 12: How Often Do You Do the Laundry?


## Review 3 (Units 9–12)

### Soal 6
**Pertanyaan:** Dodi says, "He can swims very fast." What is wrong with his sentence?

A. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
B. He should say, "He can swim very fast." After "can" we use the base form of the verb, without -s.
C. He should say, "He cans swim very fast."
D. He should say, "He can swimming very fast."

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Setelah "can" harus memakai kata kerja bentuk dasar (base form), tanpa akhiran -s atau -ing (Unit 9). Jadi yang benar adalah "He can swim very fast", bukan "can swims" atau "can swimming". Pilihan C salah karena "cans" bukan kata kerja bantu yang benar.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 3 (Unit 9: He's Good at Surfing)

### Soal 7
**Pertanyaan:** Read about the season.

"The leaves turn yellow and fall from the trees. The weather is cool, and people wear jackets."

What season is it?

A. Summer
B. Spring
C. Autumn
D. Winter

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Daun yang menguning dan gugur dari pohon serta cuaca yang sejuk adalah ciri musim gugur (autumn/fall) (Unit 10). Summer itu panas dan cerah, spring itu lebih hangat dan bunga tumbuh, winter itu dingin dan bersalju — ketiganya tidak cocok dengan daun yang berguguran.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 3 (Unit 10: How Much Snow Is There?)

### Soal 8
**Pertanyaan:** Rina asks Budi, "Do you know how to play chess?" Budi says, "Yes, I do. First, we ____."

Which is the best answer for Budi?

A. put the pieces on the board
B. kick the ball into the goal
C. run around the field
D. swim in the pool

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Catur (chess) adalah permainan papan (board game) untuk dua pemain — langkah pertama adalah menata bidak-bidak di atas papan (Unit 11). Pilihan B dan C cocok untuk sepak bola, D cocok untuk berenang — ketiganya bukan cara bermain catur.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 3 (Unit 11: Do You Know How to Play Chess?)

### Soal 9
**Pertanyaan:** Mother asks Sinta, "How often do you do the laundry?" Sinta says, "I do the laundry every Sunday." How often does Sinta do the laundry?

A. Once a year
B. Never
C. Every day
D. Once a week

**Jawaban benar:** D

**Pembahasan:** Sinta mencuci pakaian setiap hari Minggu (every Sunday) — artinya satu kali dalam seminggu, yaitu "once a week" (Unit 12). "Once a year" berarti satu kali setahun, "never" berarti tidak pernah, dan "every day" berarti setiap hari — ketiganya tidak cocok dengan "every Sunday".

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 3 (Unit 12: How Often Do You Do the Laundry?)

### Soal 10
**Pertanyaan:** Read about Budi.

"Budi is good at playing chess. He plays chess every Saturday."

Which sentence is true about Budi?

A. "Budi is good at chess and plays it once a week."
B. "Budi can't play chess."
C. "Budi plays chess every day."
D. "Budi is good at swimming."

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Soal ini menggabungkan tiga unit. Dari Unit 9: Budi pandai bermain catur ("good at playing chess"). Dari Unit 11: catur adalah permainan papan yang ia mainkan. Dari Unit 12: ia bermain setiap hari Sabtu (every Saturday), artinya satu kali seminggu ("once a week") — bukan setiap hari, jadi pilihan C salah. Pilihan B bertentangan dengan fakta bahwa ia pandai bermain catur, dan D salah karena tidak ada yang menyebut berenang.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 3 (Unit 9: He's Good at Surfing + Unit 11: Do You Know How to Play Chess? + Unit 12: How Often Do You Do the Laundry?)
