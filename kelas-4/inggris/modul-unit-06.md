## Unit 6: You Must Be on Time

### Soal 1
**Pertanyaan:** Class starts at seven o'clock. Rina arrives at school at seven o'clock. Budi arrives at half past seven. Who follows the school rule "You must be on time"?

A. Budi, because he arrives at half past seven.
B. Rina, because she arrives at seven o'clock, when class starts.
C. Both of them, because they both come to school.
D. Nobody, because the rule is about homework.

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Aturan "You must be on time" berarti kamu harus datang tepat waktu. Kelas mulai jam tujuh tepat (seven o'clock), jadi Rina yang datang jam tujuh mengikuti aturan itu. Budi datang jam setengah delapan (half past seven), jadi ia terlambat.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 6: You Must Be on Time

### Soal 2
**Pertanyaan:** Dodi says, "You mustn't listen to the teacher in class." What is wrong with his sentence?

A. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
B. He should say "You mustn't come to school."
C. He should say "You must listen to the teacher", because "mustn't" means "do not do it", and listening to the teacher is a rule.
D. He should say "You must shout in class."

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Kata "mustn't" berarti "tidak boleh" — larangan. Mendengarkan guru adalah kewajiban di kelas, jadi kalimat yang benar memakai "must" (harus): "You must listen to the teacher." Dodi salah memakai "mustn't" untuk sesuatu yang wajib dilakukan.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 6: You Must Be on Time

### Soal 3
**Pertanyaan:** Look at the clock. It is half past seven. Class starts at seven o'clock. What does it mean for Sinta, who is still at home?

A. She is late, because class started thirty minutes ago and she must hurry to school.
B. She is on time, because half past seven is seven o'clock.
C. She is early, because class starts later.
D. She is free today, because class is finished.

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** "Half past seven" berarti jam setengah delapan (07.30). Kelas mulai jam tujuh tepat (seven o'clock), jadi jam sudah lewat 30 menit dari waktu mulai — Sinta terlambat dan harus segera berangkat ke sekolah.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 6: You Must Be on Time

### Soal 4
**Pertanyaan:** In class, Rani raises her hand before she speaks. Dodi shouts the answer without raising his hand. Who follows the school rules, and why?

A. Dodi, because shouting shows he knows the answer.
B. Both of them, because they both answer the question.
C. Nobody, because there is no rule about speaking in class.
D. Rani, because the rule is "You must raise your hand" and "You mustn't shout in class".

**Jawaban benar:** D

**Pembahasan:** Aturan di kelas antara lain "You must raise your hand" (kamu harus mengangkat tangan) dan "You mustn't shout in class" (kamu tidak boleh berteriak di kelas). Rani mengikuti kedua aturan itu, sedangkan Dodi melanggarnya karena berteriak tanpa mengangkat tangan.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 6: You Must Be on Time

### Soal 5
**Pertanyaan:** What time is it? The clock shows nine o'clock.

A. It is half past nine.
B. It is nine o'clock.
C. It is a quarter past nine.
D. It is ten o'clock.

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Jam menunjukkan pukul sembilan tepat, jadi dalam Bahasa Inggris dibaca "It is nine o'clock." "Half past nine" berarti setengah sepuluh, "a quarter past nine" berarti sembilan lewat seperempat, dan "ten o'clock" berarti jam sepuluh tepat.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 6: You Must Be on Time
