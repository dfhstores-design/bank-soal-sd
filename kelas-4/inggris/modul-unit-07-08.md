## Unit 7: What Do You Want to Be?

### Soal 1
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Read the dialogue.

Rina: "What do you want to be?"
Budi: "____"

Which is the best answer for Budi?

A. "I want to be a teacher."
B. "I am nine years old."
C. "My name is Budi."
D. "I like apples."

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Pertanyaan "What do you want to be?" menanyakan cita-cita, jadi dijawab dengan "I want to be a/an + nama pekerjaan", misalnya "I want to be a teacher". Pilihan B menjawab umur, C menjawab nama, D menjawab kesukaan — ketiganya tidak menjawab pertanyaan tentang cita-cita.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 7: What Do You Want to Be?

### Soal 2
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Dodi says, "I want to be an doctor." What is wrong with his sentence?

A. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
B. He should say "I wants to be a doctor."
C. He should say "I want to be a doctor." "Doctor" starts with a consonant sound, so we use "a", not "an".
D. He should say "I want to be doctor."

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Kata "doctor" diawali bunyi konsonan (d), jadi memakai "a": "a doctor". Kata "an" dipakai sebelum kata yang diawali bunyi vokal, misalnya "an artist" atau "an engineer". Jadi kalimat Dodi yang benar adalah "I want to be a doctor."

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 7: What Do You Want to Be?

### Soal 3
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Sinta works in a hospital. She helps the doctor take care of sick people. What does Sinta want to be?

A. A farmer
B. A nurse
C. A pilot
D. A singer

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Orang yang bekerja di rumah sakit dan membantu dokter merawat orang sakit disebut "nurse" (perawat). "Farmer" bekerja di sawah atau ladang, "pilot" menerbangkan pesawat, dan "singer" bernyanyi — ketiganya tidak cocok dengan deskripsi Sinta.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 7: What Do You Want to Be?

### Soal 4
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Read about two children.

Rani: "I want to be a teacher because I like helping children learn."
Budi: "I want to be a pilot because I like flying in the sky."

Who wants to work at school?

A. Budi, because he likes flying
B. Both of them, because they both like their jobs
C. Nobody, because they do not want to work
D. Rani, because a teacher works at school

**Jawaban benar:** D

**Pembahasan:** Rani ingin menjadi guru (teacher), dan guru bekerja di sekolah (school). Budi ingin menjadi pilot, dan pilot bekerja di pesawat terbang, bukan di sekolah. Jadi yang ingin bekerja di sekolah adalah Rani.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 7: What Do You Want to Be?

## Unit 8: Why Do You Want to Be a Singer?

### Soal 6
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Rina asks Budi, "Why do you want to be a singer?" Which is the best answer for Budi?

A. "Because I like cooking."
B. "Because I like singing."
C. "I am a singer now."
D. "Because I am nine years old."

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Pertanyaan "Why do you want to be a singer?" menanyakan alasan menjadi penyanyi, jadi dijawab dengan "Because ..." yang alasannya cocok dengan profesi itu: penyanyi bernyanyi, jadi "Because I like singing". Pilihan A alasannya cocok untuk chef (koki), C menjawab seolah Budi sudah menjadi penyanyi, D menjawab umur — ketiganya tidak menjawab pertanyaan tentang alasan.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 8: Why Do You Want to Be a Singer?

### Soal 7
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Dodi says, "I want to be a singer because I like cooking." What is wrong with his sentence?

A. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
B. He should say, "I am a singer now."
C. The reason does not match the job. A singer sings songs. Dodi likes cooking, so a chef is a better job for him.
D. He should say, "Because I like singing," without "I want to be".

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Alasan setelah kata "because" harus cocok dengan profesi yang diinginkan. Penyanyi bernyanyi, bukan memasak — jadi alasan "because I like cooking" tidak cocok untuk singer. Kalau Dodi suka memasak, profesi yang cocok adalah chef (koki).

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 8: Why Do You Want to Be a Singer?

### Soal 8
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Budi says, "I want to be a doctor because I like helping sick people." Sinta says, "I want to be a doctor because I like singing." Who gives the right reason for the job, and why?

A. Budi, because a doctor helps sick people.
B. Sinta, because a doctor sings songs for sick people.
C. Both are right, because the reason does not matter.
D. Neither is right, because a doctor does not help people.

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Budi benar karena alasan "helping sick people" (menolong orang sakit) cocok dengan pekerjaan dokter. Sinta salah karena alasan "singing" cocok untuk penyanyi, bukan dokter. Dalam kalimat "I want to be a ... because ...", alasan harus sesuai dengan profesi yang disebutkan.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 8: Why Do You Want to Be a Singer?

### Soal 9
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Sinta says, "I want to be a farmer because I like ____." Which word completes her sentence correctly?

A. teaching children
B. helping sick people
C. singing songs
D. growing rice and vegetables

**Jawaban benar:** D

**Pembahasan:** Petani (farmer) bekerja di sawah dan kebun — menanam padi dan sayuran ("growing rice and vegetables"). Pilihan A cocok untuk teacher, B cocok untuk doctor, dan C cocok untuk singer. Jadi hanya D yang alasannya cocok dengan profesi farmer.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 8: Why Do You Want to Be a Singer?
