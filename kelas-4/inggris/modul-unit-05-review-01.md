## Unit 5: What Subject Do You Like the Most?

### Soal 1
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Read the dialogue.

Teacher: "What subject do you like the most, Sinta?"
Sinta: "____"

Which is the best answer for Sinta?

A. "I like Math the most."
B. "I like Math very much yesterday."
C. "Math is my teacher."
D. "I like the most Math."

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Pertanyaan "What subject do you like the most?" menanyakan mata pelajaran yang paling disukai, jadi dijawab dengan pola "I like + nama mata pelajaran + the most", misalnya "I like Math the most." Pilihan D susunan katanya salah, B memakai keterangan waktu yang tidak cocok, dan C menjawab hal yang lain.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Unit 5: What Subject Do You Like the Most?

### Soal 2
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Dodi says, "I like the most English." What is wrong with his sentence?

A. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
B. "English" is not a school subject.
C. The words are in the wrong order. He should say, "I like English the most."
D. He should say, "I like the most in English."

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Pola yang benar adalah "I like + nama mata pelajaran + the most". Kata "the most" diletakkan di akhir kalimat, bukan di tengah. Jadi kalimat Dodi yang benar adalah "I like English the most."

### Soal 3
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Rani says, "I like Art the most. I like Science, too, but not as much as Art." Budi says, "I like Science the most." Who likes Art the most?

A. Budi, because he likes Science the most.
B. Rani, because she likes Art the most.
C. Both, because they both like Art.
D. Nobody, because Art is not a school subject.

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Rani berkata "I like Art the most" — Art adalah mata pelajaran yang paling ia sukai, walaupun ia juga suka Science. Budi paling suka Science, bukan Art. Jadi yang paling suka Art adalah Rani.

### Soal 4
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** A new friend asks you, "What subject do you like the most?" You love drawing and painting in Art class. Which sentence is correct for you?

A. "I like Art the most."
B. "I like the Art most."
C. "Art likes me the most."
D. "I most like the Art."

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Jawaban yang benar memakai pola "I like + Art + the most". Pilihan B menghilangkan "the", pilihan C membalik subjeknya (Art yang menyukai), dan pilihan D susunan katanya salah.

## Review 1 (Unit 1-4)

### Soal 6
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Read the dialogue.

Rudi: "Hello! My name is Rudi. Where are you from?"
Sari: "____"

Which is the best answer for Sari? Sari is from Bandung.

A. "I'm from Bandung."
B. "I'm nine years old."
C. "My name is Rudi."
D. "I like listening to pop."

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Pertanyaan "Where are you from?" menanyakan asal, jadi dijawab dengan "I'm from + nama tempat" — "I'm from Bandung" (materi Unit 1). Pilihan B menjawab umur, C mengulang nama Rudi, dan D menjawab kesukaan — ketiganya tidak menjawab pertanyaan tentang asal.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 1 (Unit 1–4)

### Soal 7
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** My father is tall and strong. My mother is short and kind. Who is tall and strong?

A. My mother, because she is short and kind.
B. My father, because he is tall and strong.
C. Both of them, because they are my parents.
D. Nobody, because they are short.

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Ayah dideskripsikan "tall and strong" (tinggi dan kuat), sedangkan ibu "short and kind" (pendek dan baik hati). Jadi yang tinggi dan kuat adalah ayah (materi Unit 2 — mendeskripsikan orang). Pilihan A tertukar dengan deskripsi ibu, C salah karena deskripsi keduanya berbeda, dan D salah karena ayah justru tinggi.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 1 (Unit 1–4)

### Soal 8
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Dina says, "They likes listening to jazz." What is wrong with her sentence?

A. "likes" should be "like", because the subject is "they"
B. "listening" should be "listen", because we cannot use verb-ing after "like"
C. Nothing is wrong. The sentence is correct.
D. "jazz" should be "pop", because jazz is not a kind of music

**Jawaban benar:** A

**Pembahasan:** Untuk subjek "they", kata kerjanya memakai bentuk dasar "like", bukan "likes" (bentuk -s hanya untuk he/she/it). Kalimat yang benar: "They like listening to jazz." Kata "listening" tetap memakai verb-ing setelah "like", dan jazz memang salah satu genre musik (materi Unit 3).

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 1 (Unit 1–4)

### Soal 9
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Budi went to the park last Sunday. He played football with his friends and he was very happy. Rina asks, "Did you have a good time at the park?" What does Budi answer?

A. "Yes, I do. I play football every day."
B. "Yes, I did. I played football with my friends."
C. "Yes, I am. I am happy."
D. "No, I didn't. I stayed at home all day."

**Jawaban benar:** B

**Pembahasan:** Budi pergi ke taman hari Minggu lalu dan bermain sepak bola dengan gembira, jadi jawabannya "Yes, I did" (bentuk past, karena pertanyaannya tentang kemarin) ditambah ceritanya "I played football with my friends" (materi Unit 4). Pilihan A dan C memakai bentuk sekarang ("do", "am") yang tidak cocok untuk pertanyaan tentang masa lalu, sedangkan D bertentangan dengan cerita — Budi tidak tinggal di rumah.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 1 (Unit 1–4)

### Soal 10
<!-- Metadata soal: {"status":"lolos"} -->
**Pertanyaan:** Last night, my family and I ____ a funny film at home. We laughed a lot.

Which verb is correct to complete the sentence?

A. watch
B. watches
C. watched
D. watching

**Jawaban benar:** C

**Pembahasan:** Kalimat ini bercerita tentang tadi malam ("last night"), jadi kata kerjanya harus bentuk past. Bentuk past dari "watch" adalah "watched" (materi Unit 4). Pilihan A "watch" dan B "watches" adalah bentuk sekarang, sedangkan D "watching" dipakai untuk kalimat yang sedang berlangsung, bukan untuk cerita tadi malam.

**Rujukan:** Bahasa Inggris Kelas 4, Review 1 (Unit 1–4)
